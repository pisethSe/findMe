# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Cambodian students, especially students moving from the provinces to Phnom
Penh, use FindMe on mobile devices to find affordable rentals near their school,
university, or college. Landlords maintain accurate rental supply, and platform
administrators moderate users, listings, institutions, and reports.

Student discovery is free. A new landlord receives one server-timed seven-day,
no-card trial. After Khmer-first role selection and a short owner profile, the
first-use path continues into a guided rental form; returning owners use a
clean dashboard for listings, room availability, trial status, and inquiries.

## Product Purpose

FindMe makes student accommodation easier to discover by starting with the
student's institution, then helping them compare real monthly cost, distance,
availability, amenities, and location before contacting a landlord. Success
means a student can move from choosing an institution to a useful shortlist and
inquiry without relying on scattered or stale social posts.

## Brand Personality

Practical, local, trustworthy. The experience should feel calm and immediately
useful in bright, everyday mobile conditions, with clear Khmer-first guidance
and concrete rental information rather than aspirational marketing language.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic AI-generated SaaS landing page, a broad luxury
real-estate marketplace, or a speculative prop-tech dashboard. Avoid
purple-to-blue gradients, gradient type, glassmorphism, decorative verification
claims, generic icon-card grids, low-contrast surfaces, and motion used only for
decoration.

## Design Principles

1. Start with the institution and keep the student search action unmistakable.
2. Make distance, price, availability, and freshness understandable at a glance.
3. Earn trust through specific information and real states, never fabricated
   statistics or decorative badges.
4. Design mobile-first for common Cambodian network and device conditions.
5. Treat maps and motion as progressive enhancements to an always-usable list.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, meaningful image
alternatives, high contrast, Khmer-capable typography, stable layouts, and
touch-friendly controls. Availability must not rely on color alone. The phrase
loop must expose stable accessible copy without repeated announcements and stop
for reduced-motion users. Map failure, low-power, and reduced-motion states must
retain a complete list-based discovery path.
